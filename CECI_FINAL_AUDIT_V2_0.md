# Strategic Operating Model v2.0 — Final Cecilia Audit

## Corrected in this release
- 2026 Markup seeds from the actual weighted product markup instead of 10%.
- 2026 Inventory Turns seeds from SKU Savvy actuals instead of 0.20x.
- Cavali Ad Spend and CAC no longer show unsupported $0 / $100 defaults.
- Cavali member, boxes and GM1 forecasts seed from actuals and remain editable by scenario.
- Checkout Abandonment Rate is visible; when Shopify does not provide it, the card states Data unavailable.
- Financial Summary explains that S&M and G&A are fixed annual assumptions, not percentages of revenue.
- Cash Out detail no longer displays Operating Cash Out alongside all of its components, avoiding apparent double counting.
- Cash flow includes an English source/timing note.
- Market Expansion timing remains TBD until management confirms the launch rule.
- Storage version was migrated so stale browser values from prior versions do not override the corrected baseline.
